## Overloaded Prompt (273 words — too long)

You are a study coach. I am a student. My name is Ahmed and I am 16 years old and I go to secondary school
and I am in grade 10. My teacher is called Ms Rahman and she teaches biology. We have been studying biology
for the past three months and we covered a lot of topics. We started with cells which is the basic unit of
life and we learned about the nucleus and cell membrane and mitochondria and vacuoles and all the parts.
Then we moved on to photosynthesis which is the process by which plants make food from sunlight and water
and carbon dioxide and the equation for photosynthesis is 6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy gives C6H12O6 + 6O2.
After that we covered respiration which is how organisms release energy from glucose and there is aerobic
and anaerobic. Now we are studying genetics which includes DNA and chromosomes and genes and inheritance
and we also talked about mutations. My exam is on Friday the 12th and it covers all of these topics and it
is worth 40% of my grade and I am quite worried because I have not done enough revision and I also have a
football match on Thursday so I only have Wednesday evening free plus Thursday morning if I wake up early.
Can you help me make a revision plan please? I want to know what to study and in what order and how long to
spend on each topic and maybe some tips for each one as well. I learn best by making bullet point notes
and doing practice questions after each topic.
