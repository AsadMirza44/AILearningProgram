# School Assistant Knowledge Base — Sample FAQ

Use this document as your trusted source in the Paper-Based RAG activity.
Answer the test questions first from memory, then look up the answer using this document.

---

## Timetable and Scheduling

**Q: When does the school day start and end?**
School starts at 08:00 and ends at 15:30. Period 1 begins at 08:10 after a 10-minute registration period.

**Q: How many periods are in a school day?**
There are 6 periods of 50 minutes each, with a 20-minute morning break after Period 2 and a 40-minute lunch break after Period 4.

**Q: What happens if a student arrives late?**
Students who arrive after 08:15 must sign in at the Front Office and collect a late pass before joining class.

---

## Exams and Assessments

**Q: When are end-of-term exams held?**
End-of-term exams are held in Weeks 11 and 12 of each term. The exact timetable is posted on the student portal four weeks before exams begin.

**Q: What is the passing score for most subjects?**
The standard passing score is 50%. Students who score below 40% in any subject are required to attend a support session with their subject teacher.

**Q: Can students request a re-mark of their exam paper?**
Yes. Students must submit a re-mark request form to the Exams Office within 5 school days of receiving their results. A fee of £10 applies.

---

## Homework and Assignments

**Q: What is the homework submission policy?**
Homework must be submitted by 23:59 on the due date through the class portal. Late submissions receive a 10% grade deduction per day up to a maximum of 3 days. After 3 days, the assignment receives a zero.

**Q: What happens if a student has a valid reason for missing a deadline?**
Students with a valid medical or personal reason should inform their class teacher at least 24 hours before the deadline when possible. Extensions are granted at the teacher's discretion.

---

## Technology and Devices

**Q: Are students allowed to use personal laptops in class?**
Yes, personal laptops are allowed in all classes where the teacher has given permission. Mobile phones must be kept in school bags and on silent during lessons.

**Q: What is the school's policy on AI tools for assignments?**
Students may use AI tools to support their research and planning but must disclose AI use in their submission. Submitting AI-generated text as entirely their own work is considered academic misconduct.

---

## Support and Wellbeing

**Q: How can a student access the school counsellor?**
Students can book a session with the school counsellor through the student portal or by leaving a note with their form tutor. Same-day appointments are available for urgent situations.

**Q: Where is the learning support room?**
The learning support room is on the ground floor of Block B, Room B04. It is open Monday to Thursday from 08:00 to 16:00.
