# AI or Not AI? (Human vs AI Challenge)

Use `examples.csv` as screen prompts or voting cards. Show one example at a time, let participants vote, then reveal the answer and explanation.

Suggested flow:

1. Display the example without the answer.
2. Ask participants to vote AI or Not AI.
3. Ask one or two groups to explain their clue.
4. Reveal the answer and read the concept note.
